Your Stack Card

One sheet, both sides. The front has the Stack and the numbers. The back is your card: fill it in by hand, keep it where you’ll see it, and look at it again in four weeks.

In the print window, choose Print on both sides and Flip on long edge.

Success Stack

College is your first job. What you build here sets up the next ten years.

The four blocks

  • Skills: what you can do
  • Projects: proof you can use it
  • Certifications: get you past HR
  • Internships: work in your field

The four windows

  • Resume: one page, best blocks first
  • LinkedIn: headline, education, a club
  • GitHub or portfolio: your projects
  • Posts or a blog: what you’re learning

The numbers

  • 52% of new college grads are underemployed a year after graduating.Burning Glass Institute & Strada, Talent Disrupted, 2024
  • 73% of those who start out underemployed still are ten years later.Same report
  • 48.5% lower odds of underemployment with at least one internship.Same report
  • #1 tie-breaker between equal candidates: an internship with that employer (4.5 of 5) or in its industry (4.3).NACE, Job Outlook 2026
  • 63.1% of 2024–25 interns were converted to full-time hires.NACE, 2026 Internship & Co-op Survey
  • 7.4 s is a recruiter’s average first look at a resume.Ladders eye-tracking study, 2018

Three rules for AI: never paste your SSN, student ID, address or a password · check every deadline on the official site · everything on your resume is your own work, and true.
