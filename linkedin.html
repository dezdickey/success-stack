Window 2: LinkedIn

Set up LinkedIn in ten minutes

  1. Join or sign in in the LinkedIn app or at linkedin.com. Use a personal email you’ll keep after college.
  2. Headline: [Major] student at [School] | [field you want] | [a skill] For example: Business student at [your school] | Marketing | Excel
  3. Education: your school, your degree and major, and your expected graduation year.
  4. One club you’re in, or one you’ll join this month.
  5. A photo: your face, clear and well lit. Tonight is fine.
  6. Connect with three classmates.

Connect with three classmates

  1. In the LinkedIn app, tap the search bar, then the QR code icon.
  2. Show your code to a classmate, or scan theirs. Then tap Connect.
  3. Three before you leave. They’re the start of your network.

These steps haven’t been checked click by click on every phone yet, and apps move their buttons around. If something isn’t where this says, search LinkedIn’s help pages.

Then let it grow with your stack

  • Add each block as it becomes real: a project, a certification, an internship.
