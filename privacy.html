Privacy

This site collects nothing

No data means nothing to lose, sell or leak. Here is exactly what that covers, and how you can check it yourself.

What the site doesn’t do

  • No cookies and no local storage.
  • No analytics, tracking or ads, and no accounts to make.
  • Nothing loads from other sites: no fonts, scripts, images or videos from anywhere else.
  • No forms that send anything. The prompt form only fills in the prompts on your screen.
  • Nothing goes to an AI from here. The prompts are text you copy and paste into an AI you choose.
  • Links don’t say where you came from. When you follow a link to another site, your browser isn’t told to send this page’s address.

How to check it

  • Every page sets a strict Content Security Policy. It tells your browser to load scripts, styles and images only from this site, and to make no other connections.
  • Watch it yourself. Open your browser’s developer tools, choose the Network tab, and reload: every request goes to this site.
  • Read the code. It’s plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, public on GitHub.

Where the site lives

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub delivers the pages to your browser under its privacy statement. The sites this guide links to, like LinkedIn and the sources, have their own policies.

You can also download the site and open it straight from your computer, with no internet connection at all.
